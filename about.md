## Key beliefs he repeats

- "The game is won before the pen is even used" — research > writing (course 1, 10:40).
- "The pain is the pitch": if you can describe a customer's pain better than they can, they subconsciously believe you have the solution.
- "Copy cannot create desire, it can only channel existing desire" (citing Eugene Schwartz, course 3).
- "Not everything that can be immediately measured matters" — future demand / brand work is under-measured (course 2).
- Organic = explore angles; paid = exploit them (courses 2 & 3).
- Learning is behavior change: a free workbook accompanies each course so viewers change behavior, not just consume.
- He prefers to write the copy himself (writing = thinking) but uses AI (Claude) for research, competitor analysis, and post-draft review.
