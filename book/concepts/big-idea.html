The Big Idea

From course 3 (~00:20:58).

"If you look at all the top creators or marketers in the space, they all have a big idea they use in their marketing."

Ogilvy:

"If you don't have a big idea, your advertising will pass like a ship in the night."

A big idea must be four things

  1. New — no one has said it before.
  2. Easy — one skill, one purchase.
  3. Safe — low risk, no need to quit everything.
  4. Big — it's about changing the trajectory of your life / future generations.

Case studies

De Beers — "Diamonds Are Forever"

"The most famous big idea in advertising... it completely changed culture. The reason people are obsessed with big diamond rings to display affection is because of this ad. It was new (diamond rings weren't a big thing before), easy (one purchase demonstrates your love), safe (lasts forever), and big (about the love of your life)."

Shelby Sap — "the Porsche Pilates lifestyle" (one of Jay's clients, about)

"She put a name to a feeling a lot of the women she markets to feel but couldn't articulate. New (no one talked about it), easy (learn one skill — high-ticket sales), safe (make sales on the side of your job, don't quit anything), big (change the trajectory of your life and future generations)."

How to build yours

  • Give a feeling a name and make it desirable.
  • It becomes the frame your unique mechanism sits inside and the through-line of your content multiplication system.
