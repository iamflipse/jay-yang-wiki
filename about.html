The trilogy (the 3 "Full Courses")

The three YouTube "Full Courses" he references:

  1. How To Write Words That Make People Buy (43.5 min) — the thinking side: Six P's of copy thinking, empathy maps, proof, priority/pain.
  2. How to Build a Personal Brand That People Buy From (22.9 min) — current vs future demand, PTL (Power/Trust/Likeness), content multiplication: Hall of Fame + 3 R's, ad hooks/bodies/CTAs.
  3. How to Create Ads That Make People Buy (37.2 min) — 5-step ad writing process, awareness levels, market sophistication stages, unique mechanism, big idea, media buying principles.

Key beliefs he repeats

  • "The game is won before the pen is even used" — research > writing (course 1, 10:40).
  • "The pain is the pitch": if you can describe a customer's pain better than they can, they subconsciously believe you have the solution.
  • "Copy cannot create desire, it can only channel existing desire" (citing Eugene Schwartz, course 3).
  • "Not everything that can be immediately measured matters" — future demand / brand work is under-measured (course 2).
  • Organic = explore angles; paid = exploit them (courses 2 & 3).
  • Learning is behavior change: a free workbook accompanies each course so viewers change behavior, not just consume.
  • He prefers to write the copy himself (writing = thinking) but uses AI (Claude) for research, competitor analysis, and post-draft review.

Business vehicle

  • Revenue Media Lab (revenuemedialab.com) — his agency; the "big baller" end-state CTA in the courses is to speak with his team there.
  • Client case studies referenced: Alex Hermosi (value acceleration method; $1–3M "swamp" ad, course 1) and Shelby Sap ("Porsche Pilates" big idea; high-ticket sales career, course 3).
