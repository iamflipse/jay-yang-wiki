# PTL: Power, Trust, Likeness

The framework from [course 2](../sources/course-2-personal-brand-transcript.md) for building a personal brand that creates opportunities, not just views.

> "A brand is about teaching... it's teaching the associations you want your audience to have. Bad branding is when your audience makes those associations on your behalf. Good branding is when you make those associations for your audience. It's intentional."

If the goal is to influence (change behavior), people are primarily influenced by three things:

## Power

Your ability to affect change in the world. Show you can do things.

- Visually: recording in front of a mansion, a car, a fancy watch; or in front of your headquarters.
- Verbally: "My portfolio has generated over $250M in aggregate revenue last year."
- **Power is relative** — bartender example: inside the bar the bartender has power over you (controls the drinks); step outside and it evaporates. Same with an investor at a kid's soccer game: "you're just a parent."

## Trust

> "Trust is belief with evidence. Belief without evidence is called faith."

Built when: you tell someone to do something → they do it → a good result happens → they associate the value with you.

- Example: **Martha Stewart** — for years she gave women step-by-step baking directions; women got compliments from friends/family and associated that value with Martha, so the next time she says "buy my cookbook/program," they do it.
- Tactics: give step-by-step directions ("read these 50 marketing books," "use this tool to distribute your content," "use these hooks").
- "We typically have high trust with our parents — for years we listened and got good results ('eat your veggies and you'll grow')."

## Likeness

How similar you are to your audience — the **in-group effect**: we listen more to people in the same group we identify with; favoritism toward in-group, skepticism toward out-group.

Two dimensions:
- **External**: how you talk, what you wear, how you look.
- **Internal**: beliefs and values you signal.

> "Whenever I'm doing marketing for a company, the first thing I do is get really clear on who are we targeting."

## Application

> "If you want to build an influential brand in 2026 and beyond, demonstrate power, trust and likeness over and over and over again in your content."

Reflection prompt: "What things do I need to be associated with, so that when my audience has a problem, they think of me first?"
