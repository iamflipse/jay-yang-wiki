# Empathy Map & Sales-Call Mining

The "People" P is built with an **empathy map** ([course 1](../sources/course-1-writing-words-transcript.md), ~00:05:06).

## How to draw the empathy map

Two axes:
1. **Current reality** — what the avatar is doing/seeing/hearing/smelling/feeling day-to-day. Golden rule: treat people how *you* want to be treated; but the **platinum rule** is better: "treat other people the way *they* want to be treated" — put yourself in their shoes, not them in yours.
2. **Future** — down = **fears** ("this is hell: where do they not want to end up?"); up = **goals** ("this is heaven").

He won't start writing until he has "an overabundance of material" — more context than he knows what to do with.

## Pro tip: talk to the customers (course 1, ~00:06:28)

> "The best way to research who your avatar is is to talk to your customers. Some of the best ads we've ever run is when we downloaded all of our sales calls, uploaded into AI, transcribed, identified the specific reasons why they bought, and said those exact things back to them."

Example transformations (sales-call phrase → ad hook):

| What the customer said on the call | Ad hook written from it |
|---|---|
| "I'm ready to build something that gives me more freedom, flexibility and control over my time and income" | "The most dangerous thing a woman can do is become disgustingly financially independent." |
| "I'm excited to invest in myself... escaping the 9-to-5 to have more freedom and time to live" | "The normal path will never make you a millionaire nowadays." |
| "I've been a stay-at-home mom for years... I finally have time to do something for myself" | "If you've been looking for a way to make money around your kid's schedule without starting a whole business, you need to hear this." |
| "My goal is consistent $30K months so I can leave corporate sales" | "You don't have to quit your job, move cities or blow up your whole life to make more money. You just have to learn one skill." |
| "I'm a broke and overworked Chicago girl... turning 30, ready for financial stability" | "If hard work was the answer, the donkey would own the farm." |

## The core principle

> "If you can describe someone's pain point better than they can, they'll subconsciously believe that you have the solution. **The pain is the pitch.**"
